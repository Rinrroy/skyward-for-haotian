Skyward 0.3.0 for the Xiaomi 15 Pro (`haotian`), made on the stock recovery
`OS4.0.0.19.XOBCNXM`.

Image: `skyward-v0.3.0-haotian-OS4.0.0.19.XOBCNXM.img`. Check it against
`SHA256SUMS`, then, with the phone in fastboot mode on the slot it runs from:

```sh
sha256sum --check SHA256SUMS
fastboot flash recovery skyward-v0.3.0-haotian-OS4.0.0.19.XOBCNXM.img
fastboot reboot recovery
```

## 这次新增的功能

### 1. 连上的 Wi-Fi 会记住，也可以忘掉

**网络 → 记住网络**（默认开启）。

连上一个网络时，它的 SSID、加密方式和密码会被保存下来，下次进入 recovery
时自动就能连，不用重新输一遍。开启「记住网络」时是这样存的：

- 只保存这些记录在 `/metadata/skyward/wlan`，**密码不在 settings.json 里**
- 每条记录一行：十六进制 SSID、加密方式、密码，最多 16 个网络——`/metadata`
  同时放着 vold 的数据分区密钥，这里不能无限长
- **写的时候整个文件替换**（先写 `.new`、刷盘、再改名），所以中途断电不会留下
  半个配置
- 启动时由这些记录生成 `wpa_supplicant.conf`，生成的那份只在内存里，不落到
  加密分区上

**忘记一个网络**：在网络页的「已保存的网络」里点它右边的按钮，滑动确认。忘记
会删掉它的密码并断开当前连接。匹配用的是 **SSID 的原始字节**，不是屏幕上显示
的名字——两个非 UTF-8 的 SSID 显示出来可能一模一样，按名字匹配会删错。

关掉「记住网络」这个开关会**清空全部**已保存的网络并断开，等于把保存的密码都
删了。

### 2. 文件管理可以搜索，也可以换排序

文件列表上方多了一个**搜索框**，它固定在标题栏下面，不随列表滚动；右边是一个
**排序按钮**，点一下换一种顺序（名称 → 时间 → 类型 → 名称），按钮上写着当前的
一种。

搜索框里输入什么就只留下名字含它的条目，**不区分大小写**，清空就全部回来。
键盘出现在页面底部，点「完成」收起键盘，但**不会离开文件管理**。进入另一个
文件夹时搜索框清空，不会把上一个文件夹的关键字带过去。

搜索**不只看当前这一层**：它从当前文件夹往下走，子文件夹、子文件夹的子文件夹
都搜，结果里每一行下面用小字写着它所在的文件夹（相对当前文件夹），所以同一个
名字出现在两个文件夹里也不会混。点结果里的文件直接打开它的信息页，点结果里的
文件夹就直接进那个文件夹（这时搜索框清空）。往下走的范围是有限度的：最多
2000 个文件夹、40000 个条目、200 条结果、12 层深，读不了的文件夹跳过，软链接
不跟进去——搜索跑在画界面的同一个线程里，所以它必须**一定能停**，不能因为
一个装满文件的 /data 卡住整个恢复。走得也快：遍历只看名字和“是不是文件夹”
（这两样是文件系统列目录时白给的），只有**名字对上的**条目才会去读它的大小、
时间和类型，所以每敲一个字母重新走一遍也不至于卡。

- **名称**：按名字，不区分大小写
- **时间**：修改时间新的在前，同一秒内大的文件在前
- **类型**：按文件类型分组，组内按名称

三种顺序里**文件夹永远排在最前**，而且比较是**全序**（名称相同的再按原始字节
比），所以不会有文件停在文件系统返回的任意位置上。插入 U 盘时浏览 U 盘按名称
排序。搜索结果的每一层文件夹内部也按当前这一种顺序排，换顺序时会重新搜一遍。

### 3. 系统包可以暂停下载，也可以删掉

**系统更新**页里，正在下载的包有「暂停」：暂停只是**不再写**，已经下载的部分
留在磁盘上；再点继续时从 `.part` 的真实长度接着下（`Range: bytes=<长度>-`），
不会从头再来。

已经下载好的包有「删除」，会把安装包和它的 `.part` 一起删掉。删除只认下载目录
里**单独一个文件名**——包名来自更新服务器，直接拼进路径就可能删到下载目录之外
去，所以名字里带路径分隔符或 `..` 的一律不认。

### 4. 新的关机页

**主页 → 关机**（和「重启」并排）。页面上写明关机不是重启、正在进行的安装或
备份会被中断，然后**滑动关机**。关机走的是 init 的 `sys.powerctl`。

### 5. 关闭 AVB 校验要两次确认

**高级 → AVB 校验**。关闭「启动验证」或「dm-verity」：

1. 第一次滑动之后，**不会**写入，而是进到确认页，说明关掉之后这台手机的引导器
   不再校验它要启动的系统；
2. 在确认页上再滑动一次才真正写。

以前只要一次滑动就写了。现在这条路径上**任何操作都不少于两次**：再点一次开关
只会重新进到第二步，不会绕过它。

### 6. vbmeta 可以备份，也可以写回

同一页，「留一份副本」可以把当前槽位的 `vbmeta` 存到**内部存储**或 **U 盘**的
`Skyward/vbmeta/` 下，文件名带上槽位和保存时间；列表里能看到每一份的回滚索引。

**写回之前会校验 rollback index**：副本的索引**低于**手机现在这个槽位的值时，
**拒绝写回**——写回一个更旧的 vbmeta 正是让引导器拒绝启动本槽位、手机开不了机
的原因。相同或更高则允许（写回同一份镜像不会改变引导器检查的任何东西）。没有
「强制写入」这个选项：判断不了的时候就是拒绝。

- 索引在 vbmeta 镜像的**偏移 112**。偏移 176 是 `reserved[80]`，规范要求那里
  必须是 0；照着 176 校验会变成 0 比 0，**永远放行而且看起来完全正常**。
- 写回时会**重新读一次分区现在的索引再判断一次**，不是用页面上显示的那份。
- 备份和写回的**长度是镜像本身的长度**（`256 + 认证块 + 辅助块`），不是分区的
  长度。这一条是这轮修掉的：以前两边都用「从分区开头读到的那么多」，于是在
  `boot` 分区上写回会写到分区末尾的 AVB footer 里去——而且因为存和取用的是同一
  个函数，存回来的自测是完美的。

## 关于「在 data 里删文件会回到主页」

这一版把「回到主页」这件事查清了，并且关掉了删除路径上两条能走到它的路——但**没有
拿到原始的复现步骤，所以我不能说这就是那份报告里的那一个**。如果还能再现，请把步骤
发出来（在哪一层、删的是文件还是文件夹、点一下还是滑两下），我继续查。

这类现象在本项目里只有两个来源：`run()` 返回了错误，或者发生了 panic（release 是
`panic = "abort"`）。两者都会让 init 重启 recovery，而重启的第一屏就是主页——所以
「跳回主页」就是「恢复系统崩了」，不是某个页面跳转写错了。

关掉的两条路：

1. **工作一开始就把条目拿走了。** 删除开始时 `file_job_started` 会清掉页面正在描述的
   那个条目，再推上作业页；此时任何**在清掉之前画出的帧**里发出的触摸，都会落到写着
   `.expect("the file page shows an entry")` 的地方——直接 panic。编辑、安装、重命名、
   取走、改权限、重命名页提交、删除滑动这 7 处现在都取 `Option`，取不到就写「没有可
   操作的条目」，页面原地不动。
2. **重复的请求把整轮运行结束了。** 工作还在跑的时候再来一次删除／粘贴／停止，会撞上
   效果循环里的 `ensure!(worker.is_none(), …)`，同样是 `Err` 出 `run()`。现在这三处
   改成：报一句「文件操作还在进行」、写一条 kmsg、然后继续——一个重复请求绝不能把
   已经开始的删除一起杀掉。

另外，文件列表按行取条目以前是 `.expect(...)` 再加下标，现在取不到就什么都不做：列表
在手指落下之后变短，正是这类 panic 的形状。

### 7. 更新可以走国内镜像

**设置 → 更新来源 → 国内镜像**，默认开着。

以前的更新检查和下载都是直接去 `api.github.com` 和 `release-assets.githubusercontent.com`，
这两个地址在国内经常连不上。现在**先直接访问 GitHub**，连不上时再依次试
`gh-proxy.com`、`ghfast.top`、`ghproxy.net` 这类前缀代理，以及能直接回答 API 的
`api.kkgithub.com`。镜像拿到的字节仍然要**先对得上发行版里的 SHA-256**，才会被
改名成正式文件，所以镜像给错东西只会得到一个「下载内容不对」，然后试下一个来源。

这个开关写在设置文件里，并且在**第一帧画出来之前**就生效，所以设成关闭的机器重启
后不会先偷偷试一次镜像。

### 8. 解密路径少读一遍

解密时打开 data 分区，以前对**没有屏幕锁的用户**会把这个用户的 protector（
`.pwd` / `.weaver` / `.secdis` / `spblob` 加上 locksettings 数据库的副本）读两遍：
一遍判断有没有锁，一遍在真正打开文件时又读一遍。现在只读一遍，把读到的传下去。

结果完全一样：中间没有别的 I/O，也没有别的写者，所以第二遍只能得到同一个结果或
同一个错误。审核还看了另外四个可能的优化（`/data/unencrypted/mode` 读三次、
binder 每用户重开、keystore2 每次重开数据库、`secret_note` 里一次结果被丢掉的
gatekeeper 校验），结论都是**不做**：省下的是微秒到几十毫秒，代价是把某个错误的
发现顺序挪前或删掉，也就是改变了行为。审核记录在 `target/decrypt-audit.md`。

### 9. 未压缩的 super 镜像可以直接刷入

以前从文件里选中、或用 `adb sideload` 发送一个 `super.img`，只会得到一句「没有可以安装
的东西」：镜像识别只认得 boot、vendor_boot、dtbo 和 vbmeta 这几种。现在会认出 liblp 的
几何头（偏移 4096 处的那四个字节），把它当成一整块 super 来刷。

- 目标只有 `super` 一个分区：它里面装的是两个槽位的逻辑分区，所以**不问槽位**，整块写入
- 还在合并的快照没结束时拒绝刷入，否则会把那个更新要用的分区写掉
- 写之前先按安装脚本的同一套流程把当前槽位的分区从 super 上摘下来：用旧元数据建出来的
  dm-linear 表，不能在 super 被改写的时候还挂着
- 写完**不重新映射**：下一次开机按镜像里的新元数据映射，页面上也会提示重启

安装脚本刷 super 卡住时也不再什么都不留下：脚本的每一行输出会（截到 200 字节、最多 400
行）镜像到内核日志，超过 30 秒既没有输出也没有请求时，内核日志和安装页的脚本输出里各写
一行，说明它卡了多久、pid、`/proc/<pid>/wchan`、`/proc/<pid>/stat` 里的状态，以及它最后
说的一句话。

而且不会再一直等下去：这条路径上每一个等待都没有超时——从电脑读安装包、等脚本回话、结束
时等搬运线程——所以一个在等「根本不会来的安装包」的脚本，以前会一直等到手机重启。现在只
要十分钟里既没有输出、没有请求，而且一个块都没有从安装包里读到，就结束这次安装，并把上面
那份报告当作原因：还在写的写入会让安装包一直被读，所以**不会**放弃任何还在动的安装。结束
时最多等搬运线程五秒，等不到就把它留在那里，这样即使它正卡在「电脑已经不发数据」的那次读
里，已经结束的安装也仍然能把结果说出来。

### 10. 刷 super 快了很多

一块 9 GB 的 super 以前要两三分钟，原因有两个：写之前没有告诉闪存那一段数据不要了，所以
闪存得先把旧块搬走才能写新块；写完还把**整份**镜像读回来比对，9 GB 的镜像就是 18 GB 的
读写。TWRP（YARP 就是它）不这么做：写之前 discard、一遍写完、最后 fsync、完全不读回。

现在照这个做法：

- 写之前先 discard 要写的整段范围。用的是普通的 `BLKDISCARD`，**不是**安全擦除：9 GB 的
  安全擦除本身就是一次很慢的擦除，而这一段马上要被全部覆盖，擦得再干净也没有意义
- 一次写 4 MiB，而不是 1 MiB
- 写完的比对按大小分开：256 MiB 以内的镜像（boot、init_boot、vendor_boot、dtbo、vbmeta）
  照旧整份读回来比 SHA-256；更大的只读回开头、中间、结尾各 1 MiB，和写的时候算出的哈希比。
  **代价是长镜像中间没被读回的那些部分，写坏了不会发现**——这是拿完整性换速度，
  `WINDOW`（窗口大小）和 `VERIFY_MOST`（整份读回的上限）就是这两个数
- 刷手机上的文件（文件页面里选中的镜像）时，镜像直接从那个文件读，不再绕一遍 Skyward
  自己的 FUSE 服务：以前每个 64 KiB 都要走一次内核请求和一次 SHA-256，9 GB 就是十几万次
  来回，光这一项就要一分钟。电脑端 sideload 过来的包没有本地文件，仍然走 FUSE
- 每写 1 GiB 往内核日志写一行速率，比如
  `super: 4096 of 9216 MiB, 812 MB/s, reading 1100 MB/s, writing 900 MB/s`；写完再写一行
  汇总，把 discard、flush、比对各自花了多久也写出来。下次再慢，看这两行就知道时间花在
  读、写、还是等闪存上

安装脚本那条路（`7za e -so ... | zstd -c -d > /dev/block/by-name/super`）同样改了两处：
命令写出的东西先攒到 4 MiB 再交给内核，结束时 flush 并把目标 fsync 一次，让脚本写的东西在
紧接着的重启之前就已经落到闪存里，而不是等内核回写；每 1 GiB 也写一行速率。

## This release in English

**A joined Wi-Fi network is kept.** With the remember switch on, a network joined
from the WLAN page is remembered by its SSID bytes, its security and its
password, in `/metadata/skyward/wlan` — not in the settings file, at most 16
networks because `/metadata` also holds vold's key for the data partition, and
the whole file replaced at once (write `.new`, flush, rename) so a start cut off
mid-write cannot leave half a configuration. The supplicant's config is
generated from those records into RAM at every start. A kept network can be
forgotten from its row, which drops the password and disconnects it, and
turning the switch off clears them all.

**The file list searches, and the order is one button.** A search box sits above
the list, pinned under the bar so scrolling cannot take it away, with the order
button to its right: one tap turns name → time → type → name and the label says
which one is on. Typing keeps only the names that contain it, ignoring case;
clearing brings everything back; Done puts the keyboard away without leaving the
file manager; another folder starts from an empty box. Name, time or type, and
every order is total — time is newest first, type groups by file type, ties fall
to the lowercased name and then the raw one — so no entry is left where the
filesystem happened to return it. Folders stay first in all three.

**The search looks below the folder, not just in it.** It walks the folders under
the one on the page, all the way down, and each row says in small type which
folder its entry is in, counted from the folder searched, so the same name in two
folders cannot be confused. A file found opens on its own page and every action
there uses the path it was found at, not one joined to the folder on screen; a
folder found goes into that folder and empties the box. The walk is capped — 2000
folders, 40000 entries, 200 results, 12 levels deep, unreadable folders skipped and
links never followed into — because it runs on the same thread that draws the
screen: a search must always be able to stop rather than hang a full `/data`
partition. It is cheap as well as short: the walk reads names and whether an
entry is a folder, both of which the filesystem hands over with the listing, and
only the entries it is going to show are looked at closely for size, time and
type, so a keystroke can walk the tree again without the box feeling slow. Each
folder's own hits come in the current order, and turning the order walks the
tree again.

**A download can be paused and deleted.** A pause is the `.part` left on disk,
which is also what the next fetch resumes from, using the file's real length as
the offset. Deleting takes the package and its `.part`, and only by a name that
is exactly one path component: the package's name comes from the update server,
and joining it to a path unchecked would let it delete outside the download
folder.

**A power-off page.** Home → Power off, beside Reboot: what it interrupts is
written out, and a swipe does it through init's `sys.powerctl`.

**Turning AVB off takes two confirmations.** The first swipe no longer writes:
it goes to a page saying what a bootloader that checks nothing means, and a
second swipe there writes. No path through the page is fewer than two acts.

**vbmeta can be kept and given back.** A copy of the running slot's image is
kept in `Skyward/vbmeta/` on internal storage or a USB drive, listed with its
rollback index. A copy is written back only if that index is not below the one
the slot holds now — writing an older one back is exactly what makes a
bootloader refuse to start the slot — and the slot's index is read again at the
moment of the write. There is no override: what cannot be judged is refused.
The index is at offset 112 of the image; 176 is reserved and must be zero, so a
guard reading there compares 0 with 0 and allows everything while looking
correct. Copies are the image's own length, not the partition's — which is one
of the two bugs this work found: keeping and writing back both used the same
"as much as was read from the partition's start", so they agreed with each
other and a round trip was perfect while on a `boot` partition the write landed
in the AVB footer.

**Updates can go through a mirror in the mainland.** Settings → Updates →
Mirror in the mainland, on by default. The check and the download used to go
straight to `api.github.com` and `release-assets.githubusercontent.com`, which
often cannot be reached from there. GitHub is still asked first; only when it
does not answer are `gh-proxy.com`, `ghfast.top`, `ghproxy.net` and the API host
`api.kkgithub.com` tried, in that order. Whatever a mirror returns still has to
match the release's SHA-256 before it is renamed into place, so a mirror handing
over the wrong bytes only yields "the download is damaged" and the next source.
The switch is in the settings file and is applied before the first frame, so a
phone that turned it off does not quietly try a mirror at the next start.

**The decryption path reads one thing once.** Opening the data partition used to
read a lockless user's protector — `.pwd`, `.weaver`/`.secdis`, the `spblob`
files and a copy of the locksettings database — twice: once to see whether there
is a lock, once inside the open. It is read once and passed down now. The result
is the same because there is no I/O between the two reads and no other writer,
so the second could only have returned the same protector or the same failure.
The audit also looked at four other candidates — `/data/unencrypted/mode` read
three times, the binder reopened per user, keystore2 reopening its database per
user, and a gatekeeper verify in `secret_note` whose result is discarded — and
turned all four down: each saves microseconds to tens of milliseconds by moving
where an error is found or by deleting one, which is a different behaviour. The
audit is at `target/decrypt-audit.md`.

**On "deleting inside `/data` goes back to the home page".** This release works
out what that symptom *is* — an `Err` or a panic out of the run loop restarts the
recovery, and a restart begins at the home page — and closes the two ways the
delete path could reach it. It is **not** claimed as fixed: without the original
steps I cannot say the reporter's case was one of these. Two things were closed:

1. **Starting the work takes the entry away.** `file_job_started` clears the entry
   the page was describing and puts the work's page up, so a touch that arrives
   from a frame drawn just before that found nothing and panicked on
   `.expect("the file page shows an entry")`. Seven such places — edit, install,
   rename, pick up, permissions, the rename page's own commit, and the delete
   swipe — now map the entry to an `Option` and say "there is no entry left to
   act on".
2. **A repeated request ended the run.** A second delete, paste or stop arriving
   while the first was still going hit an `ensure!` in the effect loop, which
   ended the run the same way. Those three now report that work is under way and
   keep going: a request asked for twice must not take down the work the first
   one began.

Reading a list row by index was closed on the way as well: it used to be
`.expect(...)` plus an index, and now a row that is no longer there draws nothing.

**A raw super image is a package of its own.** Picking a `super.img` as a file, or
sending it with `adb sideload`, used to answer "nothing this recovery installs":
image detection knew boot, vendor_boot, dtbo and vbmeta only. It knows liblp's
geometry header now — four bytes at offset 4096 — and writes the whole partition,
with no slot to pick, because super holds the logical partitions of both slots.
A super write is refused while a snapshot is still merging, which would lose the
partitions that update needs; the running slot's tables are released from super
first, the way an install script gets them, since a dm-linear table built from the
metadata being replaced must not be mounted or mapped while it is written. Nothing
is mapped again afterwards: the next start maps what the image says, and the page
says to restart.

An install script that goes quiet no longer leaves nothing behind either. Its
output is mirrored into the kernel log, 200 bytes of a line and 400 lines of it,
and after 30 seconds of neither output nor requests the kernel log and the page's
script output both say how long it has been quiet, its pid, where it waits
(`/proc/<pid>/wchan`), its state and the last line it printed — a phone stuck here
cannot be asked for a log afterwards, so it says what it was doing while it is
still stuck.

And it no longer waits for ever. Every wait on the install path is without a
deadline — a read of the package from the computer, a reply from the script, the
serving thread at the end — so a script left waiting on a package that is not
coming waited until the phone was restarted. Ten minutes with no output, no
request and not one block of the package served now ends the install instead,
with that same report as the reason: a write that is going on keeps the package
being read, so nothing is given up on that was still moving. The serving thread
is waited on for five seconds when the package is unmounted and then left behind,
so an install that has ended can say how it ended even when the thread is stuck
in a read from a computer that has stopped sending.

**A super image is written much faster.** Nine gigabytes took two or three
minutes, for two reasons: nothing told the storage that the range was about to be
written over, so it had to copy every old block away before writing the new one,
and the whole image was read back and compared afterwards — eighteen gigabytes of
I/O for a nine-gigabyte image. TWRP (which is what YARP is) does neither: it
discards the range, writes once, syncs, and never reads back.

This now does the same. The range about to be written is discarded first, with
the plain `BLKDISCARD` and not the secure one: a secure discard of nine gigabytes
is itself a slow erase, and clearing spare blocks as well buys nothing over a
range every byte of which is written over at once. Writes are four megabytes at a
time instead of one. What is read back depends on the size: an image of 256 MiB
or less — boot, init_boot, vendor_boot, dtbo, vbmeta — is still read back whole
and compared by SHA-256, and a larger one only in a megabyte at its start, its
middle and its end, against the hash taken as those bytes were written. The cost
is that a long image written badly between those windows goes unseen: that is
certainty traded for speed, and `WINDOW` and `VERIFY_MOST` are the two numbers
that set it. Every gigabyte written adds a line to the kernel log with the rate
it went at, `super: 4096 of 9216 MiB, 812 MB/s, reading 1100 MB/s, writing
900 MB/s`, and a last line once the write is over gives the whole time and how
much of it went on discarding, flushing and checking. The next slow flash then
says whether it waited on reading, on writing, or on the storage itself.

One more thing was slow, and it was not the storage: an image the phone holds was
read back through Skyward's own FUSE server, one 64 KiB block per kernel request
with a SHA-256 taken of each, so nine gigabytes meant a hundred and fifty
thousand round trips — a minute of nothing but that. Such a file is now read where
it lies; a package that comes from a computer still goes through the mount,
because there is no file to read instead.

The install-script route is changed in two ways as well — a script that runs
`7za e -so ... | zstd -c -d > /dev/block/by-name/super` — a command now gathers
what it writes into four megabytes before handing it over, and flushes and syncs
its destination once when it ends, so what a script wrote is in the flash before
the restart that follows rather than waiting for the kernel to write it back; it
adds a rate line every gigabyte too.
